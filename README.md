# 📊 Sales Performance Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
This Power BI project analyzes sales performance across four different regions to assist management in identifying top-performing managers and areas of concern regarding profitability. The goal was to move beyond simple reporting and provide actionable strategic insights.

## 🛠 Tools Used
- **Power BI:** Data Modeling, DAX Measures, and Visualization
- **Power Query:** ETL (Extract, Transform, Load) and Data Cleaning
- **Excel:** Raw Data Source (Superstore Sales 2015)

## 📊 Key Insights
- **🏆 Revenue Leaders:** **Erin** leads the team with **$592K** in sales (East Region), closely followed by **William** with **$526K** (West Region).
- **⚠️ Profitability Warning:** The *Sum of Profit by Region* chart highlights a critical issue in the **South Region**, which is currently operating at a net loss, unlike the other three profitable regions.
- **👥 Customer Analysis:** The **Central Region** has successfully captured the highest market share in terms of customer count but requires strategy adjustments to improve revenue per customer.

## 📉 Visuals Used
1.  **Donut Chart:** To show the percentage contribution of sales by region.
2.  **Bar Chart:** To compare customer volume across regions.
3.  **Area Chart:** To visualize the sharp profit drop-off in the South region.
4.  **Cards & Matrix:** For high-level KPIs (Total Sales, Returns) and detailed manager performance.

## 🚀 Conclusion & Recommendations
The dashboard reveals that while revenue is healthy in the East and West, **immediate intervention is required in the South Region** to stop profit leakage. Additionally, the Central Region presents a prime opportunity for revenue growth through **upselling campaigns** to its large existing customer base.
